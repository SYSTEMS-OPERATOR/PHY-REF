quadric decimation, preserving source world coordinates, labels and material
base colors. The manifest identifies changes and source hashes. Normals,
textures and vertex colors are not retained in previews. No correction,
mirroring or canonical rescaling is applied. No endorsement or anatomical,
medical, biomechanical or fabrication qualification is implied.
